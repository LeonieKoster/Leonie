You have enough. Why doesn't it feel that way?

We always want more. But where does that desire come from? And when does "more" actually become enough?

MORE

  • money.
  • likes.

  • attention.
  • experiences.
  • productivity.
  • life.

BUT WHY

We think we’ll be happier if we have more. More likes, a better body, a better life. We’re constantly chasing the next thing, believing that once we achieve it, we’ll finally feel satisfied. But as soon as we get what we wanted, the goalposts shift again. There’s always something else to improve, something more to achieve, or something new to desire.

What felt like enough yesterday suddenly feels like too little today. We compare ourselves to others, set new expectations, and keep looking for ways to become a better version of ourselves. Without even noticing, we turn happiness into something we have to earn instead of something we can experience along the way.

Why is it so difficult to feel satisfied with what we already have? Why does more so often leave us wanting even more? And what would happen if we stopped chasing the next thing for a moment and asked ourselves: What if we already have enough?
